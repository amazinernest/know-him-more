# Know Him More: The Complete 365-Day Character Study Curriculum
> **Curriculum Goal**: To know God more through the lives, seasons, struggles, and redemption of biblical men and women across the entire biblical storyline from Genesis to Revelation.
## Curriculum Summary Statistics
- **Total Days**: 365
- **Total Bible Chapters Assigned**: 730 chapters (exactly 2 chapters per day)
- **Unique Memory Verses**: 365 distinct verses (no repeats across the entire year)
- **Bible Translation**: World English Bible (WEB) / King James Version (KJV) — 100% public domain
- **Thematic Progression**: Chronological narrative arc from Genesis creation to Revelation's eternal paradise.
